# Open Graph Images — Drop Zone

Used by social cards (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Slack, WhatsApp link previews) and email previews.

**Format:** PNG (preferred for crisp text) or WebP
**Dimensions:** 1200×630 (Open Graph standard 1.91:1)
**Max file size:** 300 KB

## Required at launch

| Filename | Used by | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `og-default.png` | All pages without a custom og:image (home, contact, etc.) | ⏳ Pending — `_placeholder-og.svg` used until delivered |

## Design direction

JARA brand-compliant per §22:
- Navy `#062B49` dominant
- Wordmark "JARA INTERNATIONAL INC." in white Montserrat Bold (must be readable at 100×52 thumbnail size in feeds)
- Tagline "Global Sourcing. Built on Trust." in steel-blue subtext
- Three-panel symbol motif accent
- NO orange/red/green/yellow
- Single focal subject, plenty of negative space (Twitter/X crops to square sometimes)
